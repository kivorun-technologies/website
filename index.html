BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Can I ask for help with a small problem?

Yes. A broken form, a software issue, or advice on a computer upgrade is a good place to start. You don’t need a large project to get in touch.

How is the price decided?

Pricing depends on the work involved. We review your request first, then agree on the scope and quote before you commit.

Is support remote or on-site?

Some software and IT tasks can be handled remotely. For hardware or on-site requests, include your general area so we can confirm whether the work can be arranged.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us the service you need, a short description of the issue, and any deadline. Screenshots can help, but remove passwords and private information before sending them.
