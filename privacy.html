Browsing this website

This website has no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or newsletter subscriptions. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; the hosting provider may process ordinary connection information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Preparing an enquiry

The enquiry builder prepares a draft in your browser. It does not submit your information to a website database or save it in browser storage. Opening your email app or copying the draft happens when you choose that action.

Sending an email

When you send an email, it is delivered to the Kivorun business Gmail inbox. The email providers involved process that message. Only include the information needed to discuss your request.

External links

LinkedIn, Instagram and GitHub links open the respective platforms. Their own privacy settings and policies apply when you visit them.
